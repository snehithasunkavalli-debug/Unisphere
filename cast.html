Interstellar - Principal Cast & Crew

Directed by Christopher Nolan | Written by Jonathan Nolan & Christopher Nolan

Matthew McConaughey
Joseph Cooper
A widowed former NASA pilot turned farmer who leads the Endurance expedition into the wormhole to save humanity from impending extinction.
Anne Hathaway
Dr. Amelia Brand
NASA biologist and astronaut on the Endurance mission, driven by empirical scientific investigation and belief in Plan B colonization.
Jessica Chastain
Murphy "Murph" Cooper (Adult)
Cooper's daughter who grows to become a brilliant NASA physicist working with Professor Brand to solve the fundamental gravitational equation.
Michael Caine
Professor John Brand
High-ranking NASA scientist, director of the Lazarus missions, and creator of the gravitational equation intended to lift humanity off Earth.
Matt Damon
Dr. Hugh Mann
NASA's most celebrated scientist who transmits false optimistic data from a barren ice planet due to mortal isolation and survival instinct.
Bill Irwin
TARS (Voice & Puppetry)
Former military tactical robot with customizable humor and honesty parameters who descends into the black hole singularity to gather quantum data.
