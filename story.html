Interstellar: Plot & Scientific Foundations

Astrophysics Consulted by Nobel Laureate Kip Thorne

The Blight & The Lazarus Missions

In the mid-21st century, severe crop blight and recurring dust storms threaten human civilization with planetary starvation. Secret remnants of NASA identify a spherical wormhole near Saturn placed by a mysterious five-dimensional intelligence ("They"), providing passage to a distant galaxy with candidate habitable worlds orbiting Gargantua.

Gravitational Time Dilation (Miller's Planet)

Due to the extreme proximity of Miller's planet to the supermassive black hole Gargantua, immense gravitational curvature warps spacetime: 1 hour on Miller's surface equals 7 years on Earth. When colossal tidal waves delay Cooper and Brand's return to the Endurance, 23 Earth years elapse in minutes.

The Singular Black Hole & The Tesseract

To acquire the critical quantum gravity data needed to lift human space stations off Earth, Cooper plunges into Gargantua. Instead of death by spaghettification, he enters a 5-dimensional tesseract constructed inside the event horizon, where time is rendered as a physical spatial dimension.

Quantum Gravitational Communication

Using gravity as the only fundamental force capable of transcending dimensions, Cooper modulates the second hand of his daughter Murph's wristwatch using Morse code. Deciphering the quantum equation, adult Murph completes the gravitational propulsion theory, saving civilization.
